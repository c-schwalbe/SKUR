In collaboration with AE Design, Lucas was part of a lead design team
and developed construction documents for this multifaceted facility.
Lucas helped manage many engineering disciplines that are involved in
a project of this scale and led a crucial role in the completion of
this community oriented project.

The Butterfly House offers diverse educational opportunities,
featuring adaptable indoor and outdoor classrooms, a greenhouse,
expansive educational gardens, a plant clinic, a cutting-edge
demonstration kitchen, and a wet lab, alongside conference and
administrative spaces. Thoughtful architectural design accounts for
seasonal sun exposure and optimal site orientation to maximize natural
light while balancing private and public with indoor and outdoor
spaces to meet varied educational, experiential, and environmental
needs. The Exploration Center features Montana’s first tropical
butterfly house. Complemented by the extensive gardens and classrooms,
it offers a holistic, hands-on learning experience accessible to
people of all generations and backgrounds

Westside Retreat

Missoula, Montana

Recently remodeled 1920’s Missoula duplex. This unit features vaulted ceilings, exposed wood beams, lots of natural light and views of Mount Jumbo. The owner is an architect and carpenter creating many hand crafted details making this space truly unique. Located in Missoula’s historic Westside district with breweries, coffee shops, parks, and the Clark Fork River within walking distance. A cozy and authentic Missoula stay.

Walk past lilac hedgerows and a historic stone retaining wall into a single-story contempory living area with vaulted ceilings, expansive windows, and thriving indoor plants. An open-concept plan leads you into the newly remodeled full kitchen with concrete countertops, exposed wood beams, grand center island table, and new stainless steel appliances.
